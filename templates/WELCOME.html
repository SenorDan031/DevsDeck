About DEVSDECK

DevsDeck is a OS based web application built for developers to showcase their digital identity, repositories, platforms, projects, and more in an interactive desktop-style environment !!

Build your personalized developer workspace, connect your platforms, and let your coding universe come alive.

# AIM

DevsDeck aims to be the ultimate platform for developers to create and share their unique digital identities, by providing a customizable desktop environment. Whether you're a seasoned coder or just starting out, DevsDeck is your own digital card that can be showcased in your style.

# Feature Supports

- Customizable desktop environment
- Connect and showcase coding platforms
- Display repositories, projects, and coding stats
- View other User's DevsDeck profiles
- More optimized user experience
